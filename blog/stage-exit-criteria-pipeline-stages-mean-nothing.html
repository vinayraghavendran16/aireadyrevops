Stage Exit Criteria: Why Your Pipeline Stages Mean Nothing Without Them

June 01, 2026 7 min read

Your pipeline has six stages. Your reps know the names. But ask them what it takes to move a deal from Stage 2 to Stage 3, and you will get six different answers. Without defined exit criteria, your stages are labels, not gates. And a pipeline built on labels cannot be measured, forecasted, or analyzed by any tool.

What exit criteria look like in practice

Good exit criteria are specific, observable, and verifiable. "The prospect is interested" is not a criterion. "A discovery call has been completed, the primary pain point is documented, and the prospect has confirmed the evaluation timeline" is a criterion.

Example for a five-stage B2B SaaS pipeline:

Stage 1: Discovery. Exit: discovery call completed, primary use case documented, at least one stakeholder identified.

Stage 2: Evaluation. Exit: demo completed, technical requirements documented, access to decision-maker confirmed.

Stage 3: Proposal. Exit: pricing presented, procurement process and timeline confirmed.

Stage 4: Negotiation. Exit: commercial terms agreed, legal redlines resolved, signature timeline confirmed.

Stage 5: Closed Won. Entry: signed contract received.

Enforcing criteria in Salesforce

Use Salesforce validation rules to prevent stage transitions unless the required fields are populated. Pair validation rules with guided selling paths or Lightning page layouts that highlight which fields need to be completed for the next stage. This turns enforcement into enablement rather than obstruction.

Why this matters for AI and forecasting

Every forecast model assigns probabilities to pipeline stages. Those probabilities assume that each stage represents a consistent level of deal maturity. When stages are subjective, the probabilities are meaningless. An AI forecasting tool trained on pipeline data with subjective stages will produce sophisticated-looking predictions that are no more accurate than a spreadsheet guess.

Frequently asked questions

What are stage exit criteria?

Stage exit criteria are the specific, verifiable actions or conditions that must be met before a deal can advance to the next pipeline stage. Without these criteria, stage advancement is subjective.

How do I enforce exit criteria in Salesforce?

Use validation rules that prevent stage transitions unless required fields are populated. This ensures every deal in a given stage has actually met the criteria.
